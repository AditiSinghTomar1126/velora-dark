## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an austere, avant-garde digital salon experience situated at the intersection of high fashion, architectural minimalism, and sculptural beauty. It repudiates sterile utilitarian salon booking templates in favor of a curated editorial monograph.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Discerning clientele, fashion-forward professionals, and tastemakers seeking bespoke hair, skin, and grooming treatments.
- **Atmosphere:** Private atelier, tactile luxury, bespoke intimacy, and architectural precision.
- **Aesthetic Movement:** High-fashion editorial minimalism infused with sculptural organic curves, ultra-fine hairline boundaries, and expansive negative space.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an atmospheric dark mode that mimics the mood of a dimly lit, high-end private grooming suite. 

### Role Assignments
- **Primary (`#100F0D` — Obsidian Black):** The primary canvas foundation, conveying infinite depth, intimacy, and exclusivity.
- **Secondary (`#F4E9D9` — Warm Ivory):** The dominant typographical voice and structural contrast; eliminates the clinical harshness of pure white while delivering crisp editorial legibility.
- **Tertiary (`#B89568` — Antique Gold):** Used sparingly as a precious metallic accent for interactive highlights, key badges, status focal points, and curated dividers.
- **Neutral Deep Base (`#302119` — Deep Espresso):** Applied to elevated surface containers, nested cards, and layered interactive panels.
- **Neutral Accent (`#9D8C7B` — Muted Taupe):** Reserved for low-emphasis meta-labels, secondary descriptions, and delicate hairline borders.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the high-contrast literary strokes of **Playfair Display** with the understated geometric precision of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

### Typographic Principles
- **Editorial Contrast:** Master headlines use Playfair Display set with tightened tracking and deliberate italicizations on feature nouns to introduce couture cadence (e.g., "The *Artistry* of Form").
- **Geometric Balance:** Body and navigational elements use Plus Jakarta Sans at lightweight gradations (300/400) to balance the dramatic flair of the serif headers.
- **Editorial Micro-Labels:** All system tags, categories, pricing indicators, and navigation links leverage uppercase tracking via `label-caps` to reinforce high-fashion publication aesthetics.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows an asymmetrical fluid grid, favoring intentional white space, rhythmic breathing room, and editorial column breaks over dense card grids.

### Grid & Form Factors
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with 48px canvas margins and 32px gutters. Employs offset compositions where imagery overlaps structural columns, anchoring key text blocks with ample negative space.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with 32px margins and 24px gutters. Secondary descriptive sidebars stack gracefully under lead imagery.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 20px margins and 16px gutters. Maintains editorial proportion by scaling display titles down cleanly and introducing vertical flow dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system eschews multi-level drop shadows in favor of **low-contrast hairline outlines and dark tonal layering**. 

### Surface Architecture
- **Base Canvas:** Obsidian Black (`#100F0D`).
- **Elevated Canvas:** Deep Espresso (`#302119`) at 60% opacity paired with subtle background blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) for overlays, navigation rails, and modal sheets.
- **Hairline Boundaries:** Surface demarcation relies on 1px borders using Muted Taupe (`#9D8C7B`) at 20% to 35% opacity, evoking fine bookbinding.
- **Ambient Glow:** Critical focal interactions (such as active booking slots or hero actions) emit an ultra-diffused atmospheric back-glow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 40px rgba(184, 149, 104, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The shape system balances architectural discipline with soft organic curvature. Core structural elements retain crisp, tailored lines, while specialized components use sculptural forms.

### Geometry Rules
- **Interactive Elements & Containers:** Governed by `roundedness: 1` (4px soft corners), ensuring forms and buttons feel architectural rather than bubbly.
- **Editorial Asymmetry:** Select featured imagery and hero modules utilize bespoke single-corner rounding (e.g., top-left radius set to 64px while other corners remain 4px) to mirror couture tailoring and organic salon silhouettes.
- **Separators:** Monolithic 1px hairlines with soft gradient fades at terminal edges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Atelier Button:** Solid Warm Ivory (`#F4E9D9`) fill with Obsidian Black (`#100F0D`) text, set in uppercase `label-caps`. 4px corner radius. Padding: `16px 32px`. Hover shifts background to Antique Gold (`#B89568`) with subtle easing.
- **Secondary Ghost Button:** Transparent background, 1px border in `#9D8C7B` (35% opacity), text in Warm Ivory. Hover increases border opacity to 100% and illuminates text in Antique Gold.
- **Text Link Button:** Underlined with a delicate offset 1px rule in Antique Gold; transitions into italic serif emphasis on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Treatment Filters:** Bordered pill badges with a 1px boundary in `#9D8C7B` (25% opacity), Deep Espresso surface, and text set to `label-caps`. Active states adopt an Antique Gold hairline border and warm ivory label.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Editorial Text Inputs:** Underline-only minimal fields with a 1px `#9D8C7B` bottom border, or fully enclosed fields with `#302119` backgrounds. Floating labels use `label-meta` in Muted Taupe, shifting to Antique Gold on focus. Clean, unadorned typography with no heavy outer rings.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Selection Controls:** Architectural 16px squares and circles encased in a 1px Antique Gold border. Check states reveal a solid Antique Gold inner core separated by a 2px negative gap from the outer ring.

### Cards & Service Folios
- **Treatment Portfolio Card:** Deep Espresso base tone, 1px taupe border, generous internal padding (`32px`). Features high-fashion vertical imagery with 3:4 aspect ratio, serif treatment titles, and right-aligned ivory price tags.
- **Stylist / Artisan Profile Card:** Asymmetrical image framing with a top-left organic arc (32px), accompanied by minimalist bio copy in Plus Jakarta Sans and a restrained gold monogram stamp.

### Specialized System Components
- **Editorial Booking Stepper:** Horizontal time-interval rule resembling a fine watch dial; selected time slots illuminate in Warm Ivory with an Antique Gold pin marker.
- **Service Menu Accordion:** Flush edge-to-edge list separated by 1px hairlines; expanses glide open with a cross-fade reveal of treatment notes and artisan pairings.
